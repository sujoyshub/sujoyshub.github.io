About Me

Inspiring minds from
classrooms to boardrooms.

Sujoy is a learner, speaker, author & mentor passionate about shaping the next generation of tech innovators. With over 20 years of experience in IT across organizations such as Amazon, Accenture & Temenos, he has driven innovation, operational excellence & global-scale technology transformation.

Presently, Sujoy leads Cloud Platform Engineering at Solventum, building high-performing teams & scaling cloud platforms by leveraging AI to support their global healthcare mission.

He holds a Bachelor's in Computer Science & has pursued Executive Education in Product Management from IIM Indore — and is a certified PMP, CSPO & CSM.

Beyond the terminal, Sujoy finds balance through painting, sketching, photography, blogging about tech & mindful reading — always exploring where technology meets human potential.
